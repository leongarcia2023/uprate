public domain or under the Creative Commons license named below. Changes made for the web are listed with each photo.
Photos under a ShareAlike (BY-SA) license are shared here under that same license.

The plant summaries in assets/plants.js are excerpts from English Wikipedia, used under
CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each summary links to its article.
